# Sales-Performance-Analysis
I created a dynamic Power BI dashboard to analyze sales performance and uncover business insights.
An interactive **Sales Analytics Dashboard** built using **Power BI** to provide a comprehensive view of business performance across revenue, orders, regions, categories, customer segments, shipping modes, and product-level sales.

## 🚀 Project Overview

This dashboard helps stakeholders monitor key sales metrics, identify top-performing products, analyze regional performance, and uncover business opportunities through interactive visualizations.

## 📌 Key Features

### 🔹 KPI Summary Cards

* **Total Revenue:** 2.26M
* **Total Orders:** 10K
* **Average Order Value:** 230.77

### 🔹 Category Analysis

* Sales distribution across:

  * Technology
  * Furniture
  * Office Supplies
* Easy identification of high-revenue categories.

### 🔹 Regional Performance

* Interactive map visualization displaying sales across:

  * West
  * Central
  * South
* Geographic sales insights for strategic decision-making.

### 🔹 Monthly Sales Trend

* Month-wise sales analysis.
* Detects seasonal demand patterns and sales growth trends.

### 🔹 Customer Segment Analysis

* Sales contribution from:

  * Consumer
  * Corporate
  * Home Office
* Helps understand customer purchasing behavior.

### 🔹 Shipping Mode Analysis

* Comparison of sales by shipping methods:

  * Standard Class
  * Second Class
  * First Class
  * Same Day
* Identifies preferred delivery options.

### 🔹 Product Performance

* **Top Sales Products** highlighting best-performing items.
* **Low Sales Products** identifying products requiring attention or marketing efforts.

### 🔹 Dynamic Filtering

* Interactive **Sub-Category Slicer** for detailed analysis and customized reporting.

---

## 📈 Business Insights

* Technology generates the highest revenue contribution.
* Consumer segment contributes over 50% of total sales.
* Standard Class is the most frequently used shipping mode.
* Significant sales growth observed during the final quarter of the year.
* Certain products consistently underperform and may require inventory optimization or promotional strategies.
